Features

Everything a runner
actually needs

Full Garmin Sync

67+ fields per run — pace, heart rate zones, cadence, power, ground contact time, vertical oscillation, and daily health metrics like HRV, sleep, stress, and body battery. All synced automatically.

AI Coach Chat

A multi-stage AI pipeline that classifies your intent, fetches the right data, and responds in your language. Ask about your last run, next race, or whether you should rest today.

Smart Training Plan

Your weekly plan with progress tracking — planned vs actual km, workout types, and completion status. Know exactly what's ahead and how you're doing.

Recovery & Readiness

Training load management with CTL, ATL, TSB, and acute:chronic workload ratio. Daily readiness scoring from sleep, HRV, body battery, and stress — so you push hard on the right days.

Activity Browser & Insights

Browse all your runs with filters and classification. Tap into per-km splits, HR zone breakdowns, and running dynamics. The insights engine detects trends, flags anomalies, and compares every run against your rolling averages — all pure math, no guesswork.
